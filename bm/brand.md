## ALUM visual identity
Source: alum-logo-kit.html — vectorised from the approved ALUM mark. Governs all
shared deliverables.

## Tokens
- Coral #FF6F61 · Teal #0E7C86 · Ink #15181D — the only three brand colours.
- Neutrals: paper #F7F8FA, surface #FFFFFF, line #E2E7EC, muted #5C6470,
  faint #8A929C.

## Typography
Display: Space Grotesk · Body: Inter · Mono: JetBrains Mono.

## Logo system
One master mark in seven production-ready vector forms: stacked (default),
horizontal, mark-only / favicon, monochrome ink, monochrome white, reversed colour
(colour mark + light wordmark), reversed mono white. Use reversed or mono forms on
dark or busy/low-contrast backgrounds.

## Usage rules
- Clear space ≥ the wordmark cap-height (X) on all sides; for the mark alone, 25% of
  mark height. No type, imagery, or edges in the zone.
- Minimum size: lockup 120 px / 28 mm wide; mark 24 px / 6 mm.
- Don'ts: recolour outside the three tokens; stretch/squash/rotate; add
  shadows/gradients/outlines; place the colour logo on busy/low-contrast
  backgrounds; re-trace or rebuild the mark; alter mark-to-wordmark spacing.
